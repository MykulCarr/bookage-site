Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · applies to the Bookage browser extension

The short version

Your bookmarks never leave your browser. Bookage has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Nothing you do in it is sent to us or to anyone else.

What it reads and stores

  • Your bookmarks (the "bookmarks" permission): to show them to you, suggest where they belong, and rearrange them when you press Apply. It only changes bookmarks when you ask it to, and Undo puts them back.
  • Your review choices (keep, archive, delete, folders, renames, settings): saved in the extension's own storage inside your browser ("storage" permission). They are not synced or uploaded anywhere.
  • Site icons (Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers): read from the browser's own icon cache. No network request is made for them.

Optional: link and safety checks

Only if you turn them on (you'll be asked for access to "all websites"):

  • Link checks visit each of your bookmarked pages to see whether it still works, the same way your browser would if you opened it, but without sending your cookies or logins. The only thing those sites learn is that their page was requested.
  • Safety checks download two public lists of known malware and phishing addresses, from URLhaus (abuse.ch) and OpenPhish, and compare your bookmarks against them on your device. Your bookmarks are never sent to those services.

You can leave these off; everything else works without them.

Optional: on-device AI suggestions

In browsers that include a built-in AI model (currently Chrome, on capable computers), the Ask on-device AI button lets that model suggest folders. It runs entirely on your computer: bookmark titles and addresses are given to the browser's local model and are not sent anywhere. The browser downloads its model once, the first time any extension or site uses it.

What we never do

  • Sell, share, or transfer your data. We don't receive it in the first place.
  • Use your data for advertising, credit-worthiness, or anything unrelated to sorting your bookmarks.
  • Load code from the internet: everything the extension runs ships inside it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored. Your browser's actual bookmarks stay exactly as they are at that moment.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue.
